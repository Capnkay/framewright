Stages 1 & 2

No card of their own

and that is deliberate

Stage 1 — Input Acquisition

Accepting an upload, a pasted string, or a prompt. There is no design decision here worth a page — the contract fixes the accepted formats, the size cap, and the error codes.

Stage 2 — Preprocessing

Documented inline as the first box of Card 01's own diagram. Its one hard requirement lives in the contract: the normaliser must record its transform — scale, offsetX, offsetY, width, height — into the stage-2 trace. Without it, every bbox downstream is unusable by anyone who did not write the normaliser, and they are on a different machine.
