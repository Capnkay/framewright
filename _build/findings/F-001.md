## What is wrong

The roadmap's Phase 3 split names three tracks — **studio-preview**, **api-glassbox**,
**perception** — and no track owns **generation**. So prompt-to-IR, component planning,
layout planning, content binding, IR finalisation, model orchestration, code generation
and validation all fall to `api-glassbox` by default.

That is **eleven of the thirteen subsystems on one person** (final count, all 13 cards written), alongside the endpoints, the
ID allocator, the job store, the timeline, replay, regeneration and the zip export.

## Where

`docs/ROADMAP.md`, Phase 3 track table. Surfaced while assigning owner labels to the
stage cards — eleven of thirteen had to be labelled `api-glassbox` because
nothing else fit.

## Why it matters

This is the same defect as the Generator Studio having no owner, which was caught in the
adversarial review: **work that no track claims does not stop existing, it silently lands
on whoever is nearest.** The consequence is not just overload — it is that one person
ends up unable to explain half the system to a judge, which is the outcome the whole
track structure exists to prevent.

It also distorts the board. The 78 tasks were tracked against three names, so
`api-glassbox` is carrying generation work that was never sized as part of it.

## How to reproduce

`grep -oh "Owner: [^<]*" docs/html/stages/0*.html | sort | uniq -c`

## Suggested fix

Add a fourth track, **generation**, owning: prompt-to-IR, the planning trio, IR
finalisation, the model orchestrator, code generation and assembly, and validation.
Then re-track the affected tasks in `_build/tasks.json`.

This changes a ruling the team lead made — everyone on the spine, then split three ways —
so it is theirs to decide, not the reviewer's. The alternative is to keep three tracks and
accept that `api-glassbox` is a two-person track. With six people and three to four
building at once, a fourth track is the cheaper answer.

**Note:** this finding is about the plan, not the code. Nothing is broken today, because
Phases 1 and 2 are `any` and nobody has split yet. It becomes real at H17.

## Correction and resolution — 2026-08-20

**I overstated this when I filed it, and the correction matters.**

Checking the task board rather than the card count: the Phase 3+ `api-glassbox` tasks are
genuinely endpoints, timeline, replay, regeneration and zip. The generation work lives in
**Phase 2, which is `any`** — everyone builds it together before the split. So the
*task hours* were never as lopsided as the subsystem count suggested. Severity lowered
from MAJOR to MINOR on that evidence.

What was genuinely wrong is narrower, and still worth fixing: **explanatory ownership.**
Eleven of thirteen subsystems had no named owner who would stand in front of a judge and
account for them. That is what the track structure exists to produce.

**Resolved.** A fourth track, `generation`, now owns eight of the thirteen subsystems and
is staffed with **two people**, because the honest shape of this system is that the
generation half is simply the largest part of it — renaming the track would not have
changed that. Roadmap at v1.2; stage cards, task board and team one-pager updated to match.
